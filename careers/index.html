WHO WE LOOK FOR

Requirements & Profile

We value genuine curiosity, foundational coding logic, and dedication over past corporate experience.

Academic & Coding Foundations

  • Enrolled student or fresh graduate in CSE, CS, SWE, IT, EEE, or related computing fields.
  • Working grasp of Data Structures, Algorithms, and Object-Oriented Programming (OOP).
